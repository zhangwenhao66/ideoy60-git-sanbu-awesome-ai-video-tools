## Application identity

**Application identifier:** https://zombiecouplevideo.si/#application

Afterlove accepts one couple photo or two individual portraits in a browser studio. The interface offers 10 or 30 seconds, 720p or 1080p, and 9:16, 16:9 or 1:1 output settings. The configured video workflow uses Seedance 2.5 through Kie.

**Availability checked on 2026-10-19 (Asia/Shanghai):** studio preview is available; public generation and checkout are not open. The [public service configuration](https://zombiecouplevideo.si/api/video/config) returned `ready: false`. This configuration response does not test actual video generation. Settings shown in the studio do not mean the service is currently accepting generation jobs.

The website includes a labeled 30-second demo and separately labeled external reference clips. It does not guarantee exact facial likeness or identical results for every photo.
