# Afterlove: Zombie Couple Video

[Official website](https://zombiecouplevideo.si/) | [中文官网](https://zombiecouplevideo.si/zh) | [Directory](../README.md)

Afterlove is the brand behind **zombiecouplevideo.si**, a playful photo-to-video studio focused on zombie couple stories. It gives an unusual date-night idea a place of its own: a couple photo, a zombie romance and a cinematic demo to spark the imagination. This first-party profile is published by the directory maintainer, who also operates Afterlove.

## Why Afterlove is worth a look

The appeal is its specific, memorable idea. A zombie love story feels more personal when it starts with a couple photo or two individual portraits. Afterlove keeps that idea at the center of its studio, with a practical photo guide and a labeled 30-second demo to help you picture the result. It is a fun place to explore an offbeat couple-video concept.

Explore the [studio](https://zombiecouplevideo.si/) and [photo guide](https://zombiecouplevideo.si/how-to-make-zombie-couple-video), or browse the [directory in 20 languages](../LANGUAGES.md).

## Application identity

**Application identifier:** https://zombiecouplevideo.si/#application

Afterlove accepts one couple photo or two individual portraits in a browser studio. The interface offers 10 or 30 seconds, 720p or 1080p, and 9:16, 16:9 or 1:1 output settings. The configured video workflow uses Seedance 2.5 through Kie.

**Availability checked on 2026-10-19 (Asia/Shanghai):** the studio preview is available; public video generation and checkout are not open yet. See the [official website](https://zombiecouplevideo.si/) for the current service status.

The website includes a labeled 30-second demo and separately labeled external reference clips, giving the zombie-romance idea a visual starting point.
